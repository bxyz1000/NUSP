WHO WE ARE

NUSP — Building the Leaders of Tomorrow.

NUSP (National Union For Social Progress) is a youth-driven movement dedicated to reconnecting young minds with their cultural roots while inspiring unity and responsibility towards the nation. We are not just an organization , we are a platform where ideas become action, voices become leadership, and passion becomes purpose.

Our mission is simple yet powerful: to shape a generation that thinks critically, speaks confidently, and acts responsibly. We believe real change begins when youth are aware, united, and empowered and that is exactly what we build, one assembly at a time.

~ Pranay Atkar (Founder)

YUVAAD

YUVAD - Youth Parliament: The flagship arena for debate, democracy and action.

What is Yuvaad?

A dynamic platform designed to simulate real democratic discussions and leadership experiences for students aged 15–25.

Who Can Join?

Any student aged 15–25, any background, any city.

What Will You Win?

Certificates, trophies, leadership experience, and a meaningful network.
